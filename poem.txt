Crimson whispers of passion’s fire,
Orange glows with dreams to aspire.
Golden yellow, laughter spun,
Emerald green beneath the sun.
Azure skies where calmness flows,
Indigo nights where mystery grows.
Violet shadows softly gleam,
Together they weave a radiant dream.
This short poem celebrates the vivid spectrum of rainbow colors
Across the sky where colors gleam,
Wild magical ponies chase a dream.
Galloping light on ribbons of red,
With golden hooves where angels tread.
